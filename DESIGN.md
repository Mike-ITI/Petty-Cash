# Petty Cash interface

The reference images inform a light business workspace: a stable left sidebar, compact company header, violet active states, full-width listings, transaction forms, and a separate activity rail. There was no existing UI in this checkout to preserve.

The product's defining detail is its connected financial lifecycle. One request retains its identity through review, release, actual expense accounting and replenishment. Role switching remains in the persistent preview bar.

Palette: ink #282936, violet #6B50D5, canvas #F7F8FC, white #FFFFFF, success #188268, attention #B76E1C. Typography: DM Sans with system sans fallback; tabular numerals for amounts. Layout uses a 235px navigation rail and a flexible content region, collapsing to a drawer at tablet widths. Working pages use a sticky transaction header and contextual history or guidance rail.

The UI Skills finance search matched professional minimalism and IBM Plex typography, but its marketing page composition and navy/gold palette were not applied: the supplied dashboard references take priority. Accessible semantics, validation, reduced motion, and visible focus treatment were retained.

No backend, authentication, bank connection, or permanent file storage is included. All sample operating policies are labelled in Policies & guide.
